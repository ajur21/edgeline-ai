    In progress and results

    A pick leaves the slate when its game starts and is marked here: live first, then awaiting a result, then graded with the final score and units.

    How to read it. To hit is our probability for the side, from the game data: this season’s results and margins, recent form, rest, the injury report and home edge. Edge is that probability minus the probability implied by the price shown, in points. Each pick had to clear its own bar, the model’s margin of error on that side (never under 3 points); confidence is how far past it: Medium from 2 points, High from 5. A pick is locked once posted and graded at the posted price, even if a later check finds it below its bar. Nothing is graded against other books’ prices. Once a game starts its pick moves to In progress and results, where the live line shows the score and where the pick stands; results are graded officially from final scores at the next hourly check.
