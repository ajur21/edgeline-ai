How it works

Three steps from data to a pick

A pick only happens when a book’s price implies a lower probability than the fair line.

Prices

Ingest the market

Moneyline, spread and total prices from the major U.S. sportsbooks are pulled in every day before the slate.

Fair line

Strip the vig

The sharpest book’s prices are de-vigged into a fair probability for each line. When it doesn’t price a line, the de-vigged average of the other books stands in.

Edge = fair % − implied %

Find the edge

When a book’s price implies a lower probability than the fair line, that gap is the edge. Clear 1 point and it becomes a pick; 2 is Medium, 3.5 is High.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What does "edge" mean?

Edge is the difference between the fair probability (the sharpest book’s de-vigged price for that line, or the market consensus when it doesn’t price it) and the probability implied by the best available price. A +2% edge means the fair line has the bet winning 2 percentage points more often than that one price suggests, which is roughly +4% expected value at even odds.

Does the model guarantee winners?

No. Nothing does. Even a true edge loses often; the goal is positive expected value over a large number of bets, not winning every one.

How are results tracked?

Every pick is logged with its odds at the time of publication and graded to one unit of risk. Results are shown on the performance page and never edited after the fact.

Which sportsbooks do you use for odds?

Prices come from The Odds API: Pinnacle sets the fair line, and picks are priced at DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, BetRivers, Fanatics, ESPN Bet, Bovada or BetOnline. Each pick names the book offering the best price, and that is the price it is graded against.

Is this legal where I live?

EdgeLine AI only provides analysis. Whether you can place a bet depends on the laws where you are. Check your local regulations and only bet with licensed operators.
