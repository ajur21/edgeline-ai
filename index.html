Three steps from data to a pick

A pick only happens when our probability from the game data beats what a book’s price implies. Other books’ prices never set the grade.

Game data

Build the game model

Each team’s results and margins this season, recent form, rest and back-to-backs, the injury report weighted by scoring, and home edge become a projected margin and total for every game, and from those our probability for every side.

Prices

Shop the price

Moneyline, spread and total prices from the major U.S. sportsbooks are pulled in every day before the slate. They are the number to beat and where to bet it; the market’s own probability is shown beside ours for reference only.

Questions people ask

What does “edge” mean?

Edge is the difference between our probability for the side, from the game data (this season’s results and margins, form, rest, injuries, home edge), and the probability implied by the best available price. A +3% edge means we have the bet winning 3 percentage points more often than that price suggests, which is roughly +6% expected value at even odds. Nothing is graded against other books’ prices; the market’s own number appears on each breakdown for reference only.

Does the model guarantee winners?

No. Nothing does. Even a true edge loses often; the goal is positive expected value over a large number of bets, not winning every one.

How are results tracked?

Every pick is logged with its odds at the time of publication and graded to one unit of risk. Results are shown on the performance page and never edited after the fact.

Which sportsbooks do you use for odds?

Prices come from The Odds API (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, BetRivers, Fanatics, ESPN Bet, Bovada, BetOnline), with SportsGameOdds adding more books on each pick’s line. They are where to bet it, nothing more: the grade comes from the game data, not from any book. Each pick names the book offering the best price, and its result is scored at that price. The market’s de-vigged number is shown for reference only.

Is this legal where I live?

EdgeLine AI only provides analysis. Whether you can place a bet depends on the laws where you are. Check your local regulations and only bet with licensed operators.
