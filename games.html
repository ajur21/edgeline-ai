Games, likeliest first

Today’s games come first, then later days under their own heading. Within a day, games with a side priced under our probability lead, ordered by our probability for that side. Each cell is one side: its line, the best price and the book that has it, then our probability from the game data and its edge over that price. Lean means the side cleared its own bar and is on the board (it links to its card); Watch means the edge is at least halfway to its bar. Prices are where to bet it; nothing is graded against other books’ prices.
