A game model, not a sportsbook

It is

  • A model of the game. It gives every side a probability of its own.
  • A ranking, likeliest first. Every list is sorted by that probability.

It is not

  • A sportsbook. No account and no wagers; the bet stays yours.
  • A sure thing. No outcome is guaranteed, and the record shows every loss.

The rules it keeps, and since when

  1. Graded on game data, never on other books’ prices.Nov 4, 2026
  2. Every result is published, wins and losses alike.Nov 4, 2026
  3. Posted means locked, and graded at the posted price.Nov 5, 2026
  4. A side becomes a lean only past its own margin of error.Nov 5, 2026
  5. Nothing looks bettable after its start, and nothing quietly disappears.Nov 5, 2026
  6. Lean and parlay of the day: from leans and props, locked 3:30 PM ET, never swapped.Nov 11, 2026

Where the numbers come from

Prices
The Odds API: moneylines, spreads and totals from ten books.
Props
SportsGameOdds: player prop lines, and nine books’ prices on every lean.
Games
ESPN: schedules, scores, box scores, injuries and rosters, plus the team logos and player photos.
NFL
nflverse (CC BY 4.0): play-by-play efficiency and who starts at quarterback.
NBA
The league’s official injury report, so the model knows who plays tonight.
Weather
Open-Meteo and the National Weather Service, for outdoor games.
