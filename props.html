The props board

Our leans, ranked by game day and then by our probability that the side clears the line, likeliest first. The grade comes from game data only: half is the player’s last ten games against this number, half a projection. For the NBA that is recent form and minutes, the opponent’s defence, rest and the injury report; for the NFL it is usage (targets, carries, attempts) times what he gains per touch, against the yards the defence allows to his position, home or away and the injury report. It is not graded against other books’ prices; the best price is shown only so you know where to bet it. The bar is our probability, its tick the raw hit rate. A game that has started leaves the board.

Every player

Each card opens on the prop the board ranked, with tonight’s line typed in and the reasons listed. Change the stat, the line or the span and the grade and bars recompute from the game data.

Reading a card. Season, last 5 and last 10 are the player’s averages for the stat shown; the bars are their last games, oldest to newest, green over the line and red under it. The line box starts at tonight’s posted line when there is one and you can type any other number; your line is kept on this device. How a side is graded. Only game data: half the grade is the player’s weighted hit rate against the number over the last ten games (the last five count more, and a short sample is pulled toward even), half is a projection from the last five, last ten and season averages, scaled by the minutes trend, the opponent’s points allowed, rest and the injury report, read against the player’s own game-to-game spread. High is 70%+, Medium 62%+, Low 56%+; anything closer to even is a coin flip and says so. The board lists only lines the books quote on both sides, players with at least four games logged (three in the NFL, where the projection is usage times the gain per touch against the defence) and games still to start. Nothing here is graded against other books’ prices; the price chips exist so you know where the number is best.
