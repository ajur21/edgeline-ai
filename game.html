Reading a lean. “To hit” is our probability for a side, from the game data: this season’s results and margins, form, rest, the injury report and home edge. “Implied” is the probability in the best price, and “Edge” is the difference. A side becomes a lean only when its edge clears its own bar, the model’s margin of error on it; there is one lean a game. Nothing is graded against other books’ prices.
