Reading a pick. “Fair” is the sharpest book’s de-vigged probability for the line (or the market consensus when it doesn’t price it). “Implied” is the probability baked into the best available price. “Edge” is the difference; the higher it is, the further that book sits behind the market. Confidence follows the edge: 1 point is a Lean, 2 Medium, 3.5 High.
